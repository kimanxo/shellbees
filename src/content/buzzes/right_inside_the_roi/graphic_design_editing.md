# Photo & Video Editing Fundamentals

## Overview

Photo and video editing combine technical tools, visual design, storytelling, and digital media production to transform raw visual material into polished content. This technical briefing introduces digital media, image formats, photography, graphic design, motion graphics, visual effects, audio, professional software, workflows, and modern creative technologies.

---

## Content

* **What is Digital Media?** — Digital content such as images, video, audio, animation, and graphics represented as digital data.
* **A Brief History of Digital Photography & Video** — The evolution from analog cameras and film to digital cameras, smartphones, and computer-based production.
* **Raster vs Vector Graphics** — Raster graphics use pixels; vector graphics use mathematical shapes and paths.
* **Pixels, Resolution & Aspect Ratios** — The fundamental properties that determine image detail, size, and proportions.
* **Image Properties** — Characteristics such as resolution, pixel density, color depth, and dynamic range that determine image quality.

  * **Resolution** — The number of pixels in an image.
  * **Pixel Density** — How many pixels are packed into a physical area.
  * **Color Depth** — The number of colors or tonal levels an image can represent.
  * **Dynamic Range** — The range between the darkest and brightest details that can be captured.
* **Image Color Models** — Different mathematical ways of representing color.

  * **RGB** — Red, Green, and Blue; primarily used for screens.
  * **CMYK** — Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, and Key/Black; primarily used for printing.
  * **HSV / HSL** — Models that describe color through hue, saturation, and brightness/lightness.
  * **Grayscale** — Represents images using shades from black to white.
* **Image File Formats** — Different ways of storing digital images.

  * **JPEG / JPG** — Compressed format commonly used for photographs.
  * **PNG** — Lossless format supporting transparency.
  * **GIF** — Limited-color format commonly used for simple animations.
  * **WebP** — Modern web format supporting efficient compression and transparency.
  * **AVIF** — Modern image format offering high compression efficiency.
  * **TIFF** — High-quality format commonly used in professional photography and printing.
  * **BMP** — Simple bitmap format with little or no compression.
  * **RAW** — Minimally processed camera data containing extensive image information.
  * **PSD** — Photoshop's project format supporting layers and editable elements.
  * **SVG** — XML-based vector format that scales without losing quality.
* **Image Compression** — Reducing file size by removing or reorganizing data.

  * **Lossy Compression** — Reduces file size by permanently discarding some information.
  * **Lossless Compression** — Reduces file size without permanently losing information.

### Photography

* **Exposure** — The amount of light captured by the camera sensor.
* **Shutter Speed** — Controls how long the sensor is exposed to light and affects motion blur.
* **Aperture** — Controls the lens opening and affects exposure and depth of field.
* **ISO** — Controls the sensor's sensitivity to light, with higher values generally producing more noise.
* **White Balance** — Adjusts colors so that objects appear natural under different lighting conditions.
* **Focal Length** — Determines the field of view and magnification of a lens.
* **Depth of Field** — The range of distances that appear acceptably sharp.

### Photo Editing

* **Cropping** — Removing unwanted areas of an image.
* **Resizing** — Changing the dimensions of an image.
* **Exposure & Contrast** — Adjusting brightness and the difference between light and dark areas.
* **Color Correction** — Fixing inaccurate or inconsistent colors.
* **Color Grading** — Intentionally styling colors to create a particular mood.
* **Retouching** — Removing or modifying imperfections and unwanted details.
* **Compositing** — Combining elements from multiple images into one scene.
* **Masking** — Selectively hiding or revealing parts of a layer.
* **Layers & Blending Modes** — Separating elements and controlling how they visually interact.
* **Filters & Effects** — Automated or manual visual transformations applied to media.

### Graphic Design

* **Typography** — The design and arrangement of written text.
* **Composition** — Organizing visual elements to create balance and hierarchy.
* **Color Theory** — Understanding how colors interact and influence perception.
* **Layout** — Arranging text, images, and other elements within a design.
* **Branding** — Creating a consistent visual identity for an organization or product.
* **Logos & Visual Identity** — Visual elements used to make a brand recognizable.

### Video Fundamentals

* **Frames** — Individual images that are displayed rapidly to create motion.
* **Frame Rate** — The number of frames displayed each second.
* **Resolution** — The dimensions of the video image, such as 1920×1080.
* **Aspect Ratio** — The proportional relationship between video width and height.
* **Bitrate** — The amount of data used to represent each second of video.
* **Codecs** — Algorithms used to encode and decode video or audio.
* **Containers** — File formats that package video, audio, subtitles, and metadata together.

### Video File Formats & Codecs

* **MP4** — A widely supported multimedia container.
* **MOV** — Apple's multimedia container commonly used in professional workflows.
* **MKV** — Flexible open container supporting multiple media streams.
* **AVI** — Older Microsoft multimedia container.
* **WebM** — Open container designed primarily for web video.
* **H.264** — Widely supported video codec offering good quality and compression.
* **H.265 / HEVC** — Newer codec providing better compression than H.264.
* **AV1** — Modern royalty-free codec designed for highly efficient compression.
* **ProRes** — Professional codec designed for high-quality editing workflows.

### Motion Graphics

* **Keyframes** — Points that define the value of an animated property at specific moments.
* **Animation** — Creating the appearance of movement by changing properties over time.
* **Text Animation** — Animating typography through movement, scaling, opacity, or other effects.
* **Shape Animation** — Animating vector shapes and graphic elements.
* **Motion Tracking** — Following the movement of an object so other elements can be attached to it.
* **2D Animation** — Animation using two-dimensional graphics.
* **3D Animation** — Animation using objects and environments represented in three dimensions.

### Visual Effects (VFX)

* **Green Screen / Chroma Key** — Removing a specific color, usually green, to replace the background.
* **Compositing** — Combining separately created visual elements into one final shot.
* **Rotoscoping** — Manually tracing objects or subjects to isolate them from their surroundings.
* **Masking** — Controlling which parts of a layer are visible.
* **Tracking** — Matching digital elements to the movement of real-world footage.
* **CGI** — Computer-generated imagery used to create objects, environments, or effects.

### Audio for Video

* **Voice** — Spoken dialogue or narration accompanying visual content.
* **Music** — Musical elements used to establish atmosphere and emotion.
* **Sound Effects** — Sounds added to emphasize actions or create realism.
* **Mixing** — Balancing different audio elements against each other.
* **Noise Reduction** — Removing unwanted background sounds from recordings.

### Editing Workflow

* **Import** — Bringing source media into the editing project.

* **Organization** — Structuring files, clips, assets, and folders for efficient editing.

* **Timeline** — Arranging visual and audio elements in chronological order.

* **Editing** — Cutting, rearranging, and refining media into a coherent sequence.

* **Color** — Correcting and stylizing the visual appearance.

* **Audio** — Cleaning, arranging, and balancing sound.

* **Effects** — Adding transitions, animations, filters, and visual effects.

* **Export** — Converting the project into a final media file for distribution.

* **Non-Destructive Editing** — Editing media without permanently modifying the original source files.

* **Project Files vs Exported Media** — Project files contain editable instructions and assets, while exported files are finalized media for viewing or distribution.

### Photo & Video Editing Software

* **Adobe Photoshop** — Professional image editing, compositing, and graphic design.
* **Adobe Lightroom** — Photography-focused organization, color correction, and batch editing.
* **Adobe Premiere Pro** — Professional video editing and timeline-based production.
* **Adobe After Effects** — Motion graphics, compositing, and visual effects.
* **DaVinci Resolve** — Professional video editing, color grading, audio, and visual effects.
* **Final Cut Pro** — Apple's professional video editing application.
* **Blender** — Open-source 3D modeling, animation, rendering, and visual effects software.
* **GIMP** — Free and open-source image editing software.
* **Krita** — Digital painting and illustration software.
* **Inkscape** — Open-source vector graphics editor.
* **Affinity Photo** — Professional image editing and photo manipulation.
* **Affinity Designer** — Vector and graphic design software.
* **Canva** — Browser-based design platform focused on accessible content creation.
* **Kdenlive** — Open-source non-linear video editor.
* **Shotcut** — Free and open-source video editor.

### Choosing the Right Tool

* **Photo Editing** — Photoshop, GIMP, Affinity Photo, or Lightroom.
* **Graphic Design** — Illustrator, Inkscape, Affinity Designer, or Canva.
* **Video Editing** — Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro, Kdenlive, or Shotcut.
* **Motion Graphics** — After Effects, Blender, or DaVinci Resolve Fusion.
* **VFX** — After Effects, DaVinci Resolve Fusion, or Blender.
* **3D** — Blender and other specialized 3D applications.

### Publishing & Digital Media

* **Social Media Content Creation** — Designing media specifically for platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and other digital platforms.
* **Thumbnails** — Small preview images designed to attract attention and communicate content quickly.
* **Short-form Video** — Short vertical or horizontal videos optimized for quick consumption.
* **Platform Aspect Ratios** — Choosing dimensions appropriate for different publishing platforms.
* **Exporting & Publishing** — Converting finished projects into suitable formats and distributing them.
* **Media Storage & Backup** — Organizing and protecting large collections of photos, videos, and project files.
* **Digital Asset Management** — Systematically organizing, naming, tagging, and retrieving creative assets.
* **AI in Photo & Video Editing** — Using AI for tasks such as background removal, upscaling, object removal, generation, transcription, and automated editing.
* **Ethics & Copyright** — Understanding ownership, licensing, consent, deepfakes, and responsible manipulation of media.
* **Careers in Digital Media** — Roles such as photographer, graphic designer, video editor, motion designer, VFX artist, 3D artist, and content creator.

## Key Concepts

* **Raster Image:** An image composed of individual pixels.
* **Vector Graphic:** An image represented using mathematical paths and shapes.
* **Resolution:** The number of pixels in an image or video.
* **Aspect Ratio:** The proportional relationship between width and height.
* **Codec:** Technology used to encode and decode digital media.
* **Container:** A file format that packages media streams and metadata together.
* **Bitrate:** The amount of data used to represent media per second.
* **Layer:** An independently editable visual element.
* **Mask:** A method of controlling which parts of a layer are visible.
* **Keyframe:** A point defining an animation property's value at a specific time.
* **Motion Graphics:** Animated graphic elements such as text, shapes, and logos.
* **Compositing:** Combining multiple visual elements into a single result.
* **VFX:** Visual effects created or manipulated digitally.
* **Color Correction:** Adjusting media to achieve accurate and consistent colors.
* **Color Grading:** Stylizing colors to establish a particular visual mood.
* **Non-Destructive Editing:** Editing while preserving the original source material.
* **RAW:** Minimally processed camera data containing extensive image information.
